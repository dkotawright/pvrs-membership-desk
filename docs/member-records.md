# Member records

Active members keep their cards, IDs, yearly trainings and CME current on their own page.

## For members
- On the desk, tap **My records** (sidebar, or More on a phone).
- **Keep these current:** tap **Upload new**, enter the new expiration date (or, for a training, the date you finished), and pick the photo or PDF. It shows "waiting for review" until the membership coordinator approves it.
- **CME and recertification:** log each class or drill under **Log hours**. The bars show progress toward the hours NYS requires before your EMS card expires. Attach certificates to entries.
- **My contact info:** send a new phone number, address or emergency contact.
- Reminders arrive 30 days before something expires and on the day, if alerts are on.

## For the coordinator
- **Settings → Onboard lists:**
  - **Member records:** what members keep current. Write `Name` for a card, or `Name | 12` for a yearly training.
  - **CME:** one line per category, written as `category | hours`, plus the certification period in years.
- **CME figures:** these are pre-filled from the NYS DOH CME Recertification Program for EMTs (45 hours: 20 core/refresher, 5 mandatory, 20 additional, over a 3-year period). Check them against the current DOH manual and change them here if they differ.
- **On a member's file (Onboard tab):**
  - Approve uploads.
  - Correct dates.
  - Verify CME entries.
  - Apply contact updates.
- **Compliance board (⌘K):** shows everyone at a glance and can be printed.
- **Documents:** tick "Active members' records page" to show a training handout to members.
